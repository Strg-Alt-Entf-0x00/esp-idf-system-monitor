System Monitor Studio · Architecture & Protocol

LovyanGFX Circular Gauge Architecture

The canvas renders the exact real-world display logic of system_monitor_display.cpp on a 240×240 GC9A01 circular screen:

  • Solid Gauge Arc: 270° sweep (from 135° to 405°) with 12px width (outer radius 102px, inner radius 90px).
  • Tick Scale: 20 radial ticks on the inner track with major marks every 5th step.
  • Peak & Threshold Spoke Markers: 60-sample peak line plus warning and critical limit markers.
  • Center Cluster: Single horizontal baseline with geometric degree symbol (for °C) or unit badge.
  • Dual Curved Text: Top arc (270°) displays the active metric; bottom arc (90°) displays hardware name or status, both reading naturally from Left-to-Right.
  • Offline Mode: Rotating beacon arc and disconnected monitor icon when no PC communication is received.

Serial Packet Protocol

The PC sender streams telemetry lines over serial:

  • !pc_hw_info <cpu>|<gpu>|<ram>|<ssd>|<net>: Sets hardware device names.
  • !pc_hw <seq> <cpu_t> <gpu_t> <ssd_t> <cpu_load> <gpu_load> <ram_u> <ram_tot> <vram_u> <vram_tot> <fan_cpu> <fan_case> <fan_gpu> <cpu_w> <gpu_w> <net_down> <net_up> [cores]
